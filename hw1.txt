3) Are there any variants that stand out in terms of their likely biological impact?

All variants for each dataset had IMPACT=MODIFIER, and were classified as upstream gene variants, downstream gene variants, and intergenic variants (all non-coding). LOW-HIGH VEP impact labeling signifies severity of a variant that is in a coding region. MODIFIER typically means the variant is part of a non-coding region. If there were variants predicted to alter the product (protein), this would be demarked by labels for missense, alterations in start/stop codons, frameshift, indels, etc. VEP reports on variants that fall in promoters/regulatory regions, though sometimes annotations can be wrong, and variants that lie 7 bp upstream of a gene, like ecoli_1996109_A/T or ecoli_2000253_A/T, may effect gene expression.
